Ad
Mathematics II
Amaç
This course aims to teach advanced mathematical topics such as integral calculus, series and sequences, and multivariable functions, while developing practical techniques.
İçerik
Indefinite Integral (Antiderivative), Basic Integral Rules, Method of Variable Substitution, Partial Integration, Method of Partial Fractions, Trigonometric Substitution, Reduction Formulas, Sigma Notation and Riemann Sum, Fundamental Theorems of Calculus, Basic Properties of Definite Integrals, Variable Substitution in Definite Integrals, Area Calculation, Volume Calculation, Arc Length Calculation, Surface Area Calculation, Type I Generalized Integrals, Type II Generalized Integrals, Comparison Test, Limit Comparison Test, P-Test, Absolute Convergence Test, Basic Properties of Sequences, Limit and Continuity, Convergence (Divergence) in Sequences, Basic Properties of Series, Geometric Series, Alternating Series, Convergence in Series, n-th Term Test, Comparison Test, P-Test, Ratio Test, Root Test, Integral Test, Alternating Series Test, Taylor Polynomials and Approximations, Representation of Functions as Power Series, Taylor and Maclaurin Series and Their Convergence, Introduction to Multivariable Functions, Limit and Continuity, Partial Derivatives, Chain Rule, Directional Derivative and Gradient Vector, Maximum and Minimum Values, Double Integrals, Triple Integrals

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Knows the basic integral rules and calculates integrals using appropriate integration techniques.
  2. Calculates the area under the curve, the volume of solids of revolution, the arc length, and the surface area of revolution.
  3. Evaluates generalized integrals and analyzes their convergence.
  4. Recognizes types of sequences and series and analyzes their convergence.
  5. Represents functions with power series and expands them into Taylor or Maclaurin series.
  6. Defines multivariable functions, calculates their partial and directional derivatives, and evaluates multiple integrals.
