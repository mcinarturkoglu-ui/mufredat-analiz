Ad
Artificial Intelligence
Amaç
The aim of this course is to teach students the basic principles of artificial intelligence, knowledge representation methods, search algorithms and heuristic problem solving techniques; and to provide analysis, modeling and application skills of artificial intelligence systems.
İçerik
The Artificial Intelligence course covers the theoretical foundations and applied techniques for developing intelligent systems. After a general introduction to artificial intelligence, the course covers the state space approach, search techniques (breadth-first, depth-first, A*, minimax, alpha-beta pruning), knowledge representation methods (logic, generative systems, semantic networks, framework models), and expert system architectures in detail. Topics such as problem solving strategies, heuristic approaches, and game-playing agents are supported with practical examples. In addition, areas such as natural language processing, pattern recognition, biometric recognition, introduction to machine learning, and neural networks are introduced. Throughout the semester, students analyze AI approaches suitable for different types of problems, propose models, and develop simple applications.

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Explains the concept of artificial intelligence, its historical development and basic problem-solving methods.
  2. Describes search algorithms, applies them in different scenarios, and compares them.
  3. Analyze methods of knowledge representation and develop systems of logical inference.
  4. Explains artificial intelligence application areas such as expert systems, natural language processing, and pattern recognition.
  5. Develops, codes and evaluates appropriate AI solutions for real-world problems.
