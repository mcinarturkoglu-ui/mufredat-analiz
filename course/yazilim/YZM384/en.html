Türkçe · English
Ad
Wireless Communication Networks
Amaç
To provide information about wireless networks, satellite communications, the elements used, effective ways of use and current applications.
İçerik
Working principles of wireless networks, physical properties, TCP/IP communication protocol, wireless network technologies used today and methods of establishing wireless networks.
Yöntem ve teknikler
—
Ön koşul
None
Öğretim dili
Turkish

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Provides information about the working principles of wireless networks.
  2. Explain the physical properties behind wireless communication.
  3. Provides information about TCP/IP (Transmission Control Protocol) protocol and its working structure.
  4. Explains the wireless network technologies used today.
  5. Practice setting up a wireless network.
