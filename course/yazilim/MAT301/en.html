Türkçe · English
Ad
Numerical Methods
Amaç
The aim of the Numerical Methods course is to teach students the fundamental principles and applications of numerical techniques for solving mathematical and scientific problems. This course aims to enhance students' abilities to analyze complex problems, perform mathematical modeling, and solve problems in a computer environment. Students learn various numerical methods including differential equations, integral computations, linear algebra operations, optimization, and convergence techniques, enabling them to produce mathematical solutions to real-world problems.
İçerik
The Numerical Methods course combines basic concepts of mathematics and computer science. It covers topics such as numerical solutions of differential and integral equations, fundamental principles of linear algebra, convergence and error analysis, interpolation and regression techniques, numerical integration and differentiation methods, root-finding algorithms, and the solution of linear and nonlinear optimization problems. Additionally, students gain practical experience by applying learned numerical methods to real-world problems using MATLAB or similar scientific computing software.

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Acquire the ability to solve differential and integral equations using numerical methods.
  2. Understand and apply numerical computation methods using principles of linear algebra.
  3. Develop the ability to perform error analysis and apply convergence techniques in numerical analysis.
  4. Grasp and apply fundamental numerical techniques such as numerical optimization, interpolation, and regression.
  5. Develop the ability to translate numerical methods into practical applications using MATLAB or similar scientific computing software.
