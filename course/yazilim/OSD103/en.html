Ad
Introduction to Cosmology
Amaç
The course, which is an introduction to the relationship between astronomy and cosmology, aims to present the basic knowledge and concepts in this field.
İçerik
The course start with the definition of life as we know it, then review the history of the Universe from the beginning to the emerging of the life on Earth, in a manner of cause and effect relation and from the point of view that how life could arose on the Earth and how it continue to be exist. Using this knowledge, it tries to answer the question of where life, as we know it, could be exist elsewhere in the Universe and reviews the researches and planned searches on the subject. The course ends with searches for extraterrestrial intelligence and the effects of a possible encounter with such kind of intelligence.

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Students understand the relationship of life on Earth and their own existence not only with the Earth, but with the entire Universe.
  2. Students realize that physical and chemical processes in the Universe are a result of cosmic processes.
  3. Students learns how to find an answer to the question "Are we alone in the universe?" in a rational and scientific way.
  4. Students gain insight into how they can contribute to a multidisciplinary field of study from their own discipline.
