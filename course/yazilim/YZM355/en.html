Türkçe · English
Ad
Quantum Computing
Amaç
The aim of this course is to enable students to understand the basic concepts of quantum computing, quantum algorithms and quantum computer hardware.
İçerik
This course covers the basic principles of quantum computation, starting from the limits of classical computation, qubit structure, Pauli matrices and Hilbert space. Topics such as density matrices, quantum measurement, decoherence, quantum circuits and teleportation are covered. Shor and Grover algorithms, quantum error correction, simulation, cryptography and optimization techniques are also covered.

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Explain the fundamental differences between classical and quantum computational models and understand the theoretical basis of quantum computation.
  2. The qubit structure can mathematically express quantum states in the context of Pauli matrices and Hilbert space.
  3. Density matrices can analyze quantum measurement and decoherence processes.
  4. Shor can explain quantum algorithms like Grover and discuss the areas of application of these algorithms.
  5. Describes the basic principles of quantum error correction, simulation, cryptography, and optimization techniques.
