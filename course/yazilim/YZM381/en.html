Türkçe · English
Ad
Experience Engineering
Amaç
The purpose of the Experience Engineering course is to teach students the concepts, principles, and techniques required to enhance the user experience of software products. This course aims to provide students with knowledge and skills in understanding user needs, conducting usability tests, designing interactions, and developing user interfaces.
İçerik
This course will introduce students to the concepts of user experience and usability, which will be examined in detail. Students will learn how to conduct user research, identify user needs, organize usability tests, design interactions, and create prototypes. Additionally, practical experience will be gained through user experience projects based on real-world scenarios.

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Students will gain the ability to identify user needs and evaluate user experience.
  2. Students will understand and apply fundamental concepts in interaction design and user interface development.
  3. Students will develop the ability to conduct usability tests based on real-world scenarios and evaluate the results.
  4. Students will acquire the skills to manage user research and prototyping processes.
  5. Students will be able to follow and apply current trends and technologies in interaction design and user experience.
