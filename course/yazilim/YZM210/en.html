Türkçe · English
Ad
Research Methods and Ethic
Amaç
This course aims to enable students to learn the basic concepts of science and research, to understand the scientific research process and to effectively carry out the scientific activities that need to be carried out during this process. In addition, the processes of turning scientific studies into reports, projects, theses and articles are covered and academic writing rules and research methods are taught. In addition, the concepts of ethics in scientific research, ethical rules and the principles of ethical behavior in science are emphasized, and awareness is raised on academic honesty, avoiding plagiarism, using correct sources and preventing ethical violations. Students learn to act with ethical responsibility in their academic and professional lives by adopting ethical principles in scientific studies.
İçerik
This course aims to develop students' scientific thinking and research skills by covering scientific research techniques and ethical issues together. The structure of scientific knowledge, scientific method, research process, creating research questions, literature review, data collection and scientific writing techniques are covered. In addition, ethics in science, research ethics, professional ethics and ethical problems encountered in scientific studies are discussed, and awareness is raised on academic honesty, avoiding plagiarism and preventing ethical violations. Published scientific articles are analyzed and the scientific thought process is examined, and students are provided with research projects to develop their scientific writing skills.

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Students can compare types of knowledge and distinguish their differences. Understand the definition, purpose and characteristics of scientific knowledge. Explain what scientific method, scientific research and scientific knowledge are.
  2. Acquire the skills to research, analyze, interpret and express scientific information in writing.
  3. Students can report on the difficulties experienced during the data collection process, what they learned, their suggestions regarding these processes, and fulfill the requirements of publication ethics during the process of transforming the research into publication.
  4. Students can count article types, define them, explain their features, compare their differences, and explain them.
  5. After reviewing the article critiques made in class, students can critique their own critique and revise it accordingly.
  6. Students can write a title, key concepts, and summary for an article based on the article.
  7. Describe the concepts of research and publication ethics and the responsibilities of the researcher in ensuring that a study is ethically valid and reliable.
  8. Gain the ability to design scientific research and conduct it in accordance with ethical rules.
