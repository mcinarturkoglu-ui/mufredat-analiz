Ad
Physics II
Amaç
To make students understand the basic concepts of electric and magnetism at the best level, and able to use these concepts in other courses of the department.
İçerik
-Charge and Matter - Electrik Fields - Gauss’s Law - Electric Potential - Capacitance and Dielectrics - Current and Resistance - Direct Current Circuits - Magnetic Fields -Sources of the Magnetic Field, Ampere s Law -Faraday’s Law -Alternating-Current Circuits
Yöntem ve teknikler

Öğretim dili
Turkish

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Students can calculate the electric field of charge distributions using or not using Gauss s Law.
  2. Students can calculate the electric potential of different charge distributions, and find out capaticances of capacitors in different shapes.
  3. Students can calculate the magnetic field created by the current using or not using the Biot–Savart Law and Ampere Law.
  4. Students can calculate the equivalent resistance and capaticance, and analyse complex direct current circuits applying Kirchhoff’s Rules.
  5. Students can find induced emf using Lenz and Ohm Laws, and determine the magnitude and direction of induced current.
