Ad
Guidance and Special Education
Amaç
The general aim of this course is to introduce the guidance process and special education in schools, and the main topics are: The place of guidance services in education; brief history of guidance; models and approaches to guidance; philosophy, purpose, principles and program of the developmental guidance model (comprehensive developmental guidance program); types of guidance (educational, vocational and personal guidance); the role and function of the teacher in classroom guidance; basic concepts of special education; principles and historical development of special education; legal regulations on special education; screening, referral, diagnosis and evaluation in special education; individualization of teaching; mainstreaming and support special education services; family involvement and cooperation in special education; ethical principles in guidance and special education.
İçerik
Pedagogical formation training course
Yöntem ve teknikler
Sunum, soru cevap, küçük grup çalışması.

None

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. To understand the place and importance of guidance and psychological counseling services within the contemporary school system.
  2. Have knowledge about the purposes of guidance in schools.
  3. Learns about student personality services and guidance.
  4. To understand the function of school psychological counseling and guidance service within the school.
  5. To understand the role and duties of the school counselor.
  6. Knowing the definition of special education.
  7. Knowing the types of disabilities
  8. Knowing the types of disabilities
  9. Knowing how to prepare an individualized education program.
