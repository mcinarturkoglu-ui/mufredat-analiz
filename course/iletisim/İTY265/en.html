Türkçe · English
Ad
Generative Artificial Intelligence
Amaç
The aim of this course is to provide students with the basic concepts, working principles and current application areas of generative artificial intelligence technologies from an interdisciplinary perspective. Students will be able to recognize the models used in text, visual, audio and data production, critically evaluate these technologies and adapt them to their own fields. In addition, the awareness of using artificial intelligence tools responsibly by being aware of ethical, social and professional impacts is developed.
İçerik
This course provides a basic introduction to generative AI technologies and explains how content such as text, images and audio is generated. Appealing to students from different disciplines, the course is enriched with a wide range of applications from engineering to health, from arts to social sciences. Ethics, societal impacts and use cases are also addressed to develop students' technical and critical thinking skills. Theory and practice are balanced.

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Explain the basic concepts and working principles of generative artificial intelligence.
  2. Recognize and compare artificial intelligence models that produce text, visual, audio and data.
  3. Analyzes productive artificial intelligence applications in different disciplines with examples.
  4. Generates content by applying generative AI tools in specific scenarios.
  5. Question the ethical, legal and social implications of artificial intelligence technologies.
  6. Evaluates how they can benefit from productive AI in their area of expertise.
  7. Contributes to productive artificial intelligence applications in interdisciplinary collaborative environments.
