Türkçe · English
Ad
Teaching Principles and Methods
Amaç
Basic concepts about teaching; basic approaches to teaching principles and methods; teaching strategies, methods and techniques, principles and rules for their implementation; It is a course aimed at developing the knowledge and skills of prospective teachers on the creation of the teaching environment and the suitability of tools and equipment.
İçerik
LEARNING OBJECTIVES This course aims to enable students to gain knowledge and skills regarding the basic concepts, approaches and practices of teaching principles and methods. Based on this, students are expected to achieve the following goals at the end of the education: • Defining basic concepts related to teaching principles and methods • Defining teaching principles and explaining the relationship between them. • Explaining the importance of teaching principles in the teaching process • Understand learning objectives • Explain the relationship between objectives and lesson plan • Understanding teaching strategies • Explain the relationship between learning theories and teaching • Explain teaching methods and features • Understanding teaching methods and techniques • Applying teaching techniques CALENDAR, CONTENT AND EVENTS Week 1 Course Description, Basic Concepts (source: lecture notes and ) Week 2 Curriculum Development and Curriculum Design in Education (source: lecture notes) Week 3 Learning Theories, Behaviorist (Contegrationist) Learning Theories (source: lecture notes) Week 4 Social-Cognitive Learning Theories Week 5 Cognitive Learning Theories Week 6 Humanistic and Constructivist Learning Theories Week 7 Neuro-Physiological Theory 8th Week Midterm Exam Week 9 Teaching Models and Approaches Week 10 Teaching Strategies Week 11 Teaching Methods and Techniques I Week 12 Teaching Methods and Techniques II Planning Week 13 Teaching Activities Planning Week 14 Teaching Activities 15-16.
