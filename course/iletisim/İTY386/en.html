Türkçe · English
Ad
Mitoloji
Amaç
To introduce the myths and symbols of the Middle East, Far East, Near East, taking into account their contribution to the history of art.
İçerik
Egyptian, Mesopotamian and ancient Anatolian myths; beliefs and symbols in the Greek and Roman world; Asian beliefs.

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. The student learns to interpret myths and their functions.
  2. The student comprehends mythological symbols and metaphors.
  3. Students will be able to draw analogies between the mythologies of various cultures.
  4. The student examines the place of mythology in ancient societies.
  5. The student examines the relationships between religious practices, mythology and art in ancient times.
