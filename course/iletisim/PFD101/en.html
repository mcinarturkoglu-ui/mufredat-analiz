Orijinal bilgi paketi

Türkçe · English
Ad
Introduction to Education
Amaç
To explain the fundamental concepts of education. 2. To express the similarities and differences between education and training. 3. To summarise development of educational science in Turkey chronologically. 4. To explain foundations of education.
İçerik
Fundamental concepts, * Principles of education and training, * Evaluation of educational sciences in Turkey, * Methods in educational science, * Education and philosophy, * Education and psychology, * Education and economics, * Education and sociology, * Education and politics, * Education and history
Yöntem ve teknikler
—
Ön koşul
None
Öğretim dili
Turkish

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Can understand the research methods used in education.
  2. Compares alternative perspectives in education
  3. Can explain the characteristics of the teaching profession.
  4. Can discuss the philosophical, psychological, social, economic, political and legal foundations of education
  5. Can discuss the structure and characteristics of the Turkish national education system.
