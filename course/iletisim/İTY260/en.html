Orijinal bilgi paketi

Türkçe · English
Ad
Diction
Amaç
With this course, students; to gain the skills and habit of expressing what they think, hear, see, experience, learn and know clearly, clearly, accurately and effectively; Conscious, correct and careful use of Turkish in accordance with the rules of speaking and writing; Develop the ability to express their feelings and thoughts effectively and clearly in front of a person or community, whether prepared or not; improve their ability to express themselves comfortably in business and professional life, to speak in front of the public, to make presentations; talking and solving problems they may encounter in business life; interpreting, evaluating and expressing verbally.
İçerik
It includes theoretical and practical information for effective and good speaking.
Yöntem ve teknikler
—
Ön koşul
None
Öğretim dili
Turkish

Öğrenme çıktıları

  1. Can express himself / herself comfortably and effectively in business life.
  2. Can define basic concepts and terms related to verbal expression.
  3. Prepare oral / written texts by using thought sequencing and development techniques in various verbal and written expression types.
  4. Can use Turkish grammar correctly and effectively.
  5. He / she can apply diction techniques (breathing techniques, articulation, emphasis and intonation) to speak correctly, beautifully and effectively.
  6. She can use her voice and body language effectively.
